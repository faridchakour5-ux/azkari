“Salatee” (صلاتي) is an adhan, prayer times, Quran and adhkar app: your daily companion for prayer, dhikr and memorisation — simple, elegant, with no ads and no account.

✦ Adhan and prayer times
• Prayer times based on your location, calculated on your device: in Morocco from the Ministry of Awqaf table for your nearest city, elsewhere by your country’s method.
• On Android: the adhan (athan / azan) in the muezzin’s voice when each prayer time begins, even with the app closed, the phone asleep and no Internet.
• Choose your adhan voice from fourteen muezzins (most are downloaded once).
• Qibla direction next to the upcoming prayer.
• Gentle reminders: morning and evening adhkar, salawat on the Prophet ﷺ every two hours, and a notice ten minutes before prayer — only from fifty minutes after Fajr until 10 pm.

✦ The complete Quran
• Hafs or Warsh narration (Maghrebi script), with the Tafsir al-Muyassar and your reading position resumed in one tap.
• A daily wird, khatma tracking and a reading log: how many verses you read, and when.

✦ Adhkar
• Morning and evening adhkar from the Quran and Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim, with all of Hisn al-Muslim; adhkar for sleeping, waking, travelling and more.
• A counter for each dhikr, an approximate duration per section, the virtue of dhikr and the counsels of the Prophet ﷺ.

✦ Listening and memorisation
• More than thirty reciters in Hafs, Warsh and Qalun from Nafi, stories of the prophets, the biography and authentic hadiths (Internet needed, except what you have downloaded).
• Memorise by repeating a verse or passage with three ready-made methods, plus spaced review.
• “Repeat & compare”: record your recitation and compare it with the reciter. The recording stays on your device, works only while the app is open, and is no substitute for a teacher.

✦ Learn and prepare
• Doors of goodness: twelve chapters (parents, charity, kinship, neighbours, orphans…) with hadiths from the two Sahihs and practical suggestions.
• Waqf and ibtida’ (stopping and resuming): a simple guide with examples from the Quran.
• Learn tajweed: noon sakinah and tanween, with listening and repeat-after-me for Hafs and Warsh.

✦ Muslim tools
• Qibla, Hijri calendar, prayer tracker, voluntary fasting, sleep timer and your own reminders.
• “My time”: your minutes in the app per day, week and month, counted on your device.

✦ Fiqh and quiz
• Practical fiqh made simple, and an Islamic culture quiz of more than 1,000 questions, with challenges for your friends.

✦ Tasbih and widget
• A digital tasbih (33, 99 and 100) and a prayer-times widget for your home screen (Android).

✦ Three languages, easy on the eyes
• Interface in Arabic, French and English (the Quran stays in Arabic only), white, paper or black backgrounds, adjustable text size.

✦ Privacy first
No account, no ads, no commercial tracking. Your progress stays on your device, and you can save and restore it. In the Android app your location is not sent to any server. The Quran, adhkar and tools work without Internet.

May Allah accept from us and from you.
