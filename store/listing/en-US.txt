“Salatee” (صلاتي) is your daily companion for prayer, dhikr, the Quran and memorisation — simple, elegant, with no ads and no account.

✦ Prayer times and adhan
• Times based on your location, calculated on your device: in Morocco from the Ministry of Awqaf table for your nearest city, elsewhere by your country’s method.
• On Android: the adhan in the muezzin’s voice when each prayer time begins, even with the app closed, the phone asleep and no Internet.
• Gentle reminders: morning adhkar after Fajr, evening adhkar after Asr, salawat on the Prophet ﷺ every two hours, and a notice ten minutes before prayer — all only from fifty minutes after Fajr until 10 pm.

✦ The complete Quran
• Hafs or Warsh narration (Maghrebi script), with the Tafsir al-Muyassar, your reading position remembered and resumed in one tap.
• A daily wird and khatma tracking.

✦ Adhkar
• Morning and evening adhkar from the Quran and Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim, with the option to show all of Hisn al-Muslim; adhkar for sleeping, waking, travelling and more.
• A counter for each dhikr and an approximate duration for each section, so you know whether to start now or later.
• The virtue of dhikr and the counsels of the Prophet ﷺ.

✦ Listening and memorisation
• Recitations by several reciters, stories of the prophets, the biography and authentic hadiths (Internet needed, except what you have downloaded).
• Memorise by repeating a verse or passage with three ready-made methods, plus spaced review.
• “Repeat & compare”: record your recitation, then listen to it and compare it with the reciter. The recording stays on your device and is never sent; it works only while the app is open, and it is no substitute for a teacher.

✦ Learn and prepare
• Doors of goodness: twelve chapters (honouring parents, charity, kinship ties, neighbours, caring for orphans…) with hadiths from the two Sahihs and practical suggestions, and you can tick what you did today.
• Waqf and ibtida’ (stopping and resuming): a simple guide with examples from the Quran.
• Learn tajweed: a lesson on noon sakinah and tanween, with listening and repeat-after-me for Hafs and Warsh.

✦ Muslim tools
• Qibla, Hijri calendar, prayer tracker, voluntary fasting, sleep timer and your own reminders.
• “My time”: how many minutes you spend in the app per day, week and month, counted on your device.

✦ Fiqh and quiz
• Practical fiqh made simple, and an Islamic culture quiz of more than 1,000 questions, with score sharing and challenges for your friends.

✦ Tasbih and widget
• A digital tasbih (goals of 33, 99 and 100) and a prayer-times widget for your home screen (Android).

✦ Three languages, easy on the eyes
• Interface in Arabic, French and English (the Quran stays in Arabic only), white, paper or black backgrounds, and adjustable text size.

✦ Privacy first
No account, no ads, no commercial tracking. Your progress stays on your device, and you can save it to a file and restore it. In the Android app your location is not sent to any server. The Quran, adhkar and tools work without Internet.

May Allah accept from us and from you.
